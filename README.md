### 1. Local Weyl–Heisenberg symmetry of a dimension-12 SIC-POVM

A symmetric informationally complete positive operator-valued measure (SIC-POVM) in dimension 12 has 144 outcomes. An exact construction with the standard twelve-level Weyl–Heisenberg displacements and Zauner symmetry is known. Chien and Waldron also searched for a SIC generated by displacements acting separately on two qubits and one qutrit, and found no example for the group `SmallGroup(144,197)`. Banacki et al. asked whether this local orbit could exist when studying spin correlations in Higgs decay.

**No pure state generates the required local orbit.** Fifteen Pauli overlaps would force the two-qubit reduced state to have purity 7/13, while its allowed spectrum implies purity at most 1/2. This rules out the local symmetry for every degree of entanglement and shows that the associated stabilizer Rényi entropy bound cannot be attained. The conclusion concerns a specific displacement group in dimension 12. Zauner’s conjecture concerns the standard group in every dimension.

[Banacki et al., §5.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.26122v2#S5.SS1) · [Project and results](./problems/local-sic-dimension-12/README.md) · [Paper (PDF)](./problems/local-sic-dimension-12/LocalSIC12.pdf) · [Lean proof and reproduction](./problems/local-sic-dimension-12/Lean/README.md)

### 2. Quantum error correction and the residue-field distance conjecture for stabilizer codes

Gluesing-Luerssen and Pllaha studied free stabilizer codes over finite local Frobenius rings. They proved that the code distance over the ring is at most the distance after reduction to the residue field, with equality when the reduced code is pure. **Conjecture 5.5** of their 2017 preprint proposed equality for every free code. The difficulty is deciding whether a low-weight error that changes the encoded state can become a harmless stabilizer after reduction.

A five-site counterexample has **distance 1 over the integers modulo four and distance 2 over the binary field**. Its weight-one logical error becomes a check on reduction. The paper also constructs a signed lift of the toric code. On a square torus of side length *L* ≥ 4, its binary distance is *L* but its distance at every cyclic local dimension above two is four.

[Gluesing-Luerssen and Pllaha, Conjecture 5.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.09884#page=17) · [Project and results](./problems/stabilizer-code-distance-collapse/README.md) · [Paper (PDF)](./problems/stabilizer-code-distance-collapse/DistanceCollapse.pdf) · [Lean proof and reproduction](./problems/stabilizer-code-distance-collapse/Lean/README.md)

### 3. CHSH Bell inequality and singlet extractability for pure states of Schmidt rank at most three

In a Clauser–Horne–Shimony–Holt (CHSH) Bell test, what singlet fidelity does the observed score guarantee? Bardyn et al. studied this quantitative self-testing question in 2009 and calculated the best extraction fidelity for a given pure state. Pollard later gave a formula for the maximum CHSH score of a given pure two-qutrit state. The inverse problem is harder because many different states and measurements can produce the same score.

For **pure bipartite states of Schmidt rank at most three**, the project finds the smallest Mayers–Yao fidelity compatible with each CHSH score from 2 to 2√2. This fidelity is the best squared overlap, after local isometries, with a Bell pair tensored with an auxiliary state. The sharp bound has three branches, including a plateau at **5/6** from approximately 2.468 to 2.552. At a score of 2.5 a pure two-qubit source guarantees 7/8. Admitting a third Schmidt mode lowers the guarantee to 5/6.

[Bardyn et al. on CHSH certification](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.2170#page=3) · [Project and results](./problems/chsh-bell-pair-extraction/README.md) · [Paper (PDF)](./problems/chsh-bell-pair-extraction/RankThreeCHSH.pdf) · [Lean proof and reproduction](./problems/chsh-bell-pair-extraction/Lean/README.md)

### 4. The I3322 Bell inequality for isotropic qutrit pairs: the Ito–Imai–Avis conjecture

For a maximally entangled pair of qutrits mixed with white noise, does giving each observer three binary measurement settings reveal nonlocality at a lower visibility than the two-setting CHSH test? Ito, Imai and Avis proved the CHSH threshold. Their 2006 paper reported a search with 50,000 starting points and proposed in **Conjecture 1** that I3322 has the same threshold. Proving this required an upper bound over all six local measurements.

The project derives the maximum I3322 score at every visibility α, the weight of the maximally entangled state in the mixture. **A violation is possible exactly when α > 4/(1 + 3√2)**, approximately 0.762974. The number of settings still matters. At α = 19/25, every choice of three binary settings per party admits a local model, while four explicit settings per party violate the A₅ Bell inequality by 1/16500.

[Ito, Imai and Avis, Conjecture 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0508210#page=6) · [Project and results](./problems/i3322-qutrit-conjecture/README.md) · [Paper (PDF)](./problems/i3322-qutrit-conjecture/IsotropicI3322.pdf) · [Lean proof and reproduction](./problems/i3322-qutrit-conjecture/Lean/README.md)

### 5. Quantum contextuality and the Perkel graph: a 37-dimensional witness

Liu et al. used 37 optical time-bin modes in 2025 to implement a 57-event test of GHZ-type quantum contextuality based on the Perkel graph. They asked whether fewer dimensions could attain the ideal total score of three. Constructing one rank-37 Gram matrix gave an upper bound on the dimension needed. A numerical search constrained to rank 36 found no equally good solution, but could not exclude one.

The project proves that **37 is the exact minimum complex Hilbert-space dimension** for the prescribed projective test. At the ideal score, the exclusivity relations determine every entry of the Gram matrix of the projected state vectors and force rank 37. The lower bound allows mixed states and arbitrary-rank projectors. With exact exclusivity, even a score of 3 − 27/22000 suffices to certify at least 37 dimensions.

[Liu et al., original experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11789840/) · [Project and results](./problems/perkel-contextuality/README.md) · [Paper (PDF)](./problems/perkel-contextuality/Perkel37.pdf) · [Lean proof and reproduction](./problems/perkel-contextuality/Lean/README.md)

### 6. Ergodicity of the WSK algorithm for the zero-temperature four-state Potts antiferromagnet

On a periodic triangular lattice, the ground states of the zero-temperature four-state Potts antiferromagnet are proper four-colourings. The Wang–Swendsen–Kotecký (WSK) algorithm moves between them by Kempe changes, which exchange two colours on a connected component. For width six, Mohar and Salas proved nonergodicity at heights 6, 18, 30, … and established ergodicity at height 9 by enumerating nearly 300 million colourings. Those results left infinitely many heights undecided.

For an unshifted triangular torus of width 6 and height 3*M* for a positive integer *M*, the chain is **nonergodic exactly when *M* ≡ 2 (mod 4)**. The proof uses the same 732 legal rows and 3,960 compatible row pairs at every height. Lean checks the new degree criterion, which yields the ergodicity classification together with Mohar and Salas’s published topological result. A Chinese report is available. An English paper will be added.

[Mohar and Salas on the width-six cases](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0901.1010#page=34) · [Project and results](./problems/potts-wsk-ergodicity/README.md) · [Chinese research report](./problems/potts-wsk-ergodicity/PottsSix_report_zh.md) · [Lean proof and reproduction](./problems/potts-wsk-ergodicity/Lean/README.md)

### 1. 十二维 SIC-POVM 能否由两个量子比特和一个三能级系统的局域位移生成？

十二维对称信息完备测量（SIC-POVM）有 144 个结果。对通常的十二能级 Weyl–Heisenberg 位移，已有带 Zauner 对称性的精确解。如果将系统拆成两个量子比特和一个三能级粒子（2 × 2 × 3），只对三个子系统分别施加位移，还能生成 SIC 吗？Chien 与 Waldron 在群 `SmallGroup(144,197)` 上搜索，没有找到实例。Banacki 等研究希格斯衰变的自旋关联时，也把这种局域 SIC 是否存在列为未知问题。

**所有纯初态都无法生成该局域轨道。** 只取作用在两比特上的 15 个泡利重叠条件，就能推出约化态的纯度必须等于 7/13，而它的谱又要求纯度至多为 1/2。矛盾排除了任意纠缠程度下的局域构造，也说明该研究中的稳定子 Rényi 熵上界无法达到。这里排除的是十二维的一种候选位移群，Zauner 猜想讨论的是各维数上的标准位移群。

[Banacki 等的原始问题](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.26122v2#S5.SS1) · [项目详解](./problems/local-sic-dimension-12/README.md) · [英文论文 PDF](./problems/local-sic-dimension-12/LocalSIC12.pdf) · [Lean 证明与复验](./problems/local-sic-dimension-12/Lean/README.md)

### 2. 量子纠错：自由稳定子码约化到剩余域时，码距会改变吗？

Gluesing-Luerssen 与 Pllaha 研究有限局部弗罗贝尼乌斯环上的自由稳定子码。他们证明环上的码距不大于约化到剩余域后的码距，并在约化码满足纯性条件时证明两者相等。两人在 2017 年预印本的**猜想 5.5** 中提出，所有自由码都应取等号。困难在于，原本会改变编码信息的低重量逻辑错误，经约化可能成为一个无害的稳定子。

本项目构造了五站点反例：**在模 4 整数环上码距为 1，在二元剩余域上码距为 2**。原先的单站点逻辑错误约化后恰好能由校验生成。论文还给出一族带符号提升的环面码（toric code）。对边长 *L* ≥ 4 的方格环面，二元码距为 *L*，而这一指定提升在每个大于二的循环局域维数上都只有码距 4。

[Gluesing-Luerssen 与 Pllaha 的猜想 5.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.09884#page=17) · [项目详解](./problems/stabilizer-code-distance-collapse/README.md) · [英文论文 PDF](./problems/stabilizer-code-distance-collapse/DistanceCollapse.pdf) · [Lean 证明与复验](./problems/stabilizer-code-distance-collapse/Lean/README.md)

### 3. CHSH 贝尔不等式与贝尔对提取：施密特秩至多为三时的保真度

在 CHSH 贝尔检验中，观察到一定程度的贝尔不等式违反，能保证提取出多高质量的贝尔对？这是量子自检中的定量问题。Bardyn 等在 2009 年求出了给定纯态的最优提取保真度，Pollard 后来给出了给定双三能级纯态所能达到的最大 CHSH 分数。反过来，从一个观测分数推断保真度时，必须考虑所有能产生该分数的态和测量。

对**施密特秩至多为三的双体纯态**，本项目求出了 CHSH 分数从 2 到 2√2 时，局域等距变换所能提取的贝尔对在最差情形下的平方保真度（Mayers–Yao 保真度）。精确曲线有三段，其中约 2.468 至 2.552 分这一段恒为 **5/6**。例如同样是 2.5 分，纯双比特源的保证值为 7/8，允许第三个施密特模式后便降为 5/6。

[Bardyn 等的 CHSH 认证问题](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.2170#page=3) · [项目详解](./problems/chsh-bell-pair-extraction/README.md) · [英文论文 PDF](./problems/chsh-bell-pair-extraction/RankThreeCHSH.pdf) · [Lean 证明与复验](./problems/chsh-bell-pair-extraction/Lean/README.md)

### 4. 各向同性三能级粒子对的 I3322 贝尔不等式：Ito、Imai 与 Avis 的噪声阈值猜想

在最大纠缠三能级粒子对中掺入白噪声后，把双方的可选二元测量从两项增加到三项，能否比 CHSH 检验更早检出非局域性？Ito、Imai 与 Avis 已求出 CHSH 的阈值。他们从五万个初值进行数值搜索，在 2006 年论文的**猜想 1** 中提出，I3322 的阈值与之相同。要证明猜想，需要对双方六项测量的所有可能选法给出上界。

本项目以可见度 α 表示混合态中最大纠缠态的权重，求出了每个 α 下的 I3322 最高分。**当且仅当 α > 4/(1 + 3√2)，即超过约 0.762974 时，才有测量能违反 I3322 不等式。** 在 α = 19/25 处还能严格区分测量设置的数量：每方任取三项二元测量，结果都容许局域模型；每方选用论文给出的四项测量，却能让 A₅ 贝尔不等式超过局域上界 1/16500。

[Ito、Imai 与 Avis 的猜想 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0508210#page=6) · [项目详解](./problems/i3322-qutrit-conjecture/README.md) · [英文论文 PDF](./problems/i3322-qutrit-conjecture/IsotropicI3322.pdf) · [Lean 证明与复验](./problems/i3322-qutrit-conjecture/Lean/README.md)

### 5. 量子情境性与 Perkel 图：57 个事件能否在低于 37 维的空间实现？

Liu 等在 2025 年利用 37 个光学模式实现了基于 Perkel 图的 57 事件 GHZ 型量子情境性检验，并问能否用更低维的系统达到理想总分 3。构造出一个秩为 37 的 Gram 矩阵，只能给所需最小维数一个上界。作者把秩限制为 36 后进行数值搜索，没有找到同样好的解，但也无法排除它存在。

本项目证明，对这组指定的投影测量和互斥关系，**理想得分所需的复希尔伯特空间最小维数恰为 37**。满分条件唯一确定了各投影作用于制备态后所得向量的 Gram 矩阵，其秩就是 37。即使允许混合态及任意秩的投影，下界也成立。在互斥关系严格成立时，总分达到 3 − 27/22000 就足以认证维数至少为 37。

[Liu 等的原始实验](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11789840/) · [项目详解](./problems/perkel-contextuality/README.md) · [英文论文 PDF](./problems/perkel-contextuality/Perkel37.pdf) · [Lean 证明与复验](./problems/perkel-contextuality/Lean/README.md)

### 6. 零温四态反铁磁 Potts 模型：宽六三角环面上 WSK 算法的遍历性

在零温四态反铁磁 Potts 模型中，三角晶格的基态对应合法四色着色。Wang–Swendsen–Kotecký（WSK）算法在双色连通分量上交换颜色，这种操作称为 Kempe 变换。Mohar 与 Salas 已证明宽六周期晶格在高 6、18、30……时不遍历，又通过枚举近三亿个合法着色证明高 9 时遍历。其余无穷多个高度不能靠逐个枚举来判定。

对宽 6、高 3*M*、边界无错位的三角环面，本项目证明：**WSK 链不遍历当且仅当 *M* 除以 4 余 2**，其余三的正倍数高度都遍历。证明将任意高度的环面分解成相邻行，只需统一分析 732 种合法行和 3,960 种相容行对。Lean 验证新的映射度判据，再结合 Mohar 与 Salas 已发表的拓扑结果得到遍历性分类。现有中文研究报告，英文论文将在后续添加。

[Mohar 与 Salas 的宽六问题](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0901.1010#page=34) · [项目详解](./problems/potts-wsk-ergodicity/README.md) · [中文研究报告](./problems/potts-wsk-ergodicity/PottsSix_report_zh.md) · [Lean 证明与复验](./problems/potts-wsk-ergodicity/Lean/README.md)
