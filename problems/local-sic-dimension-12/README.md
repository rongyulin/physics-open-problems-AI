# 十二维 SIC 测量的局域对称性 / Local Symmetry of SIC Measurements in Dimension 12

[中文](#中文) · [English](#english)

## 中文

### 解决了什么问题？

**两个量子比特与一个三能级系统，即使允许任意纠缠，也不能从同一个纯态出发，仅用三个子系统各自的标准 Weyl–Heisenberg 位移生成一个对称信息完备测量（SIC-POVM）。**

在十二维空间中，SIC 有 144 个秩一测量效应，对应的单位向量两两内积模平方均为 $1/13$；全部测量概率足以重建量子态。这里问的是这 144 个效应能否来自指定的局域位移轨道。把整个系统视作一个十二能级系统、用整体 Weyl–Heisenberg 位移生成 SIC，则是另一种构造，已有精确解。

### 谁提出了问题，之前卡在哪里？

- [Appleby 等](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.1055)记载了通常的十二维 Weyl–Heisenberg SIC 已有精确解。整体位移和三个子系统的局域位移各有 144 个操作（忽略整体相位），但群结构不同。
- [Chien 与 Waldron（2017，第 5 节表 2）](https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/~waldron/Preprints/NiceErrorFrames/laa.2016.11.026.pdf)较早在数值搜索中考察了局域位移群 `SmallGroup(144,197)`。表 2 标出了通常十二维位移群的数值 SIC，却没有给这个局域群标出数值解；它也没有给出不存在性证明。
- [Banacki 等（2026，第 5.1 节）](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.26122v2#S5.SS1)在研究希格斯衰变中的量子自旋关联时，**明确把这个局域 SIC 的存在性列为未知**。在他们的分析中，若存在这样的态，就能达到局域二阶稳定子 Rényi 熵的通用上界 $\log_2(13/2)$。

**当时卡住的是：**已有整体位移的精确构造，也对指定的局域位移群做过数值搜索，但还不知道有没有任意一个纯态能让局域轨道的全部重叠同时达到 SIC 要求。这里能确认的是谁较早搜索过、谁明确写下了这个开放问题，不能据此认定谁最早提出。

### 为什么重要？

**它说明，希尔伯特空间维数相同，并不意味着不同的控制操作可以实现相同的测量对称性。** 证明只需要 15 个非恒等的两比特泡利重叠条件：它们要求两比特约化态的纯度为 $7/13$，但三能级辅助系统带来的秩限制，结合代数共轭，又迫使纯度不超过 $1/2$。因此，改变初始纯态或增加三个子系统之间的纠缠，都不能消除这个障碍。

稿件还给出这 15 个重叠模偏离目标值的**可达精确最小平方误差**。不存在性定理还排除了十二维 SIC 的两种候选传递阿贝尔射影酉对称类型之一：剩下的类型等价于通常的十二能级 Weyl 位移。这并不分类所有十二维 SIC，也不解决任意维数的 SIC 存在性问题。

## English

### What problem does this solve?

**No pure state of two qubits and one qutrit, however entangled, can generate a symmetric informationally complete measurement (SIC-POVM) under the standard local Weyl–Heisenberg displacements of the three subsystems.**

In dimension 12, a SIC has 144 rank-one measurement effects. Their associated unit vectors have squared overlap $1/13$ for every distinct pair, and the outcome probabilities determine the quantum state. The question here is whether these effects can arise from the specified local orbit. Exact SICs generated by the ordinary Weyl–Heisenberg displacements on a single 12-level system were already known.

### Who raised the question, and where did prior work stop?

- [Appleby et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.1055) document the availability of exact ordinary Weyl–Heisenberg SICs in dimension 12. The ordinary and local displacement groups both have 144 elements up to phase, but have different group structures.
- [Chien and Waldron (2017, Section 5, Table 2)](https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/~waldron/Preprints/NiceErrorFrames/laa.2016.11.026.pdf) included the local displacement group, `SmallGroup(144,197)`, in an early numerical search. Their table marks a numerical SIC for the ordinary dimension-12 displacement group, but not for this local group. It gives no proof of nonexistence.
- [Banacki et al. (2026, Section 5.1)](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.26122v2#S5.SS1), in their study of quantum spin correlations in Higgs decay, **explicitly identified existence for this local group as unknown** to them. Such a state would attain the general upper bound $\log_2(13/2)$ on the local second-order stabilizer Rényi entropy in their analysis.

**What remained unresolved:** An exact construction was available for the ordinary displacement group, and the local group had been searched numerically. Whether any pure state could meet all the SIC overlap conditions on the local orbit remained unanswered. These papers document an early search and an explicit statement of the open question; they do not establish who first posed it.

### Why does it matter?

**The dimension of the Hilbert space alone does not determine which measurement symmetries a given set of controls can realize.** Just 15 nonidentity two-qubit Pauli overlaps suffice for the proof. They force the two-qubit reduced state to have purity $7/13$. The rank restriction imposed by the qutrit, combined with algebraic conjugation, forces its purity to be at most $1/2$. Thus no choice of the starting pure state or of its entanglement can remove the obstruction.

The manuscript also gives the **exact, attained minimum of the sum of squared deviations** of the 15 overlap magnitudes from their SIC target. The nonexistence theorem rules out one of the two candidate transitive abelian projective unitary symmetry types in dimension 12. The remaining type is equivalent to the ordinary 12-level Weyl displacement action. This neither classifies all dimension-12 SICs nor settles SIC existence in arbitrary dimension.
